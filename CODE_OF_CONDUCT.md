# Code of Conduct

## Our goal

We want Markflow to be a friendly place to build. Everyone can help, no matter their skill level or background.

## What we expect

Be kind. Be direct but respectful. Listen first. Assume good intent. Help others learn.

Use clear words. Avoid personal attacks. Avoid jokes that target people.

Respect different views on tools and workflows. Markflow works across stacks, so people will disagree sometimes. That is fine. Talk about the work, not the person.

## What we do not accept

We do not accept harassment, hate speech, or threats. We do not accept unwanted sexual content. We do not accept personal attacks or repeated rude behavior.

We do not accept spam or self promotion that has nothing to do with Markflow.

## If something goes wrong

If you see bad behavior, report it. Open a private report through GitHub or contact a maintainer. We will look into it and act. We will keep your report private when we can.

Maintainers can warn, mute, or remove people who break these rules. Serious cases can lead to a ban from the repo.

## Scope

These rules apply in this repo. They apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat linked from this repo.

Be kind. Build together.
